Loans for Capital Bank customers

Borrow with a bank that already knows you.

Loans are available exclusively to customers who hold an account with Capital Bank of Australia. Open your account first, then apply for a loan securely once you are logged in.

Get a Loan

Loans are for account holders

To be eligible to apply for a loan with Capital Bank of Australia, you must first be a customer, meaning you hold an open account with us. Applications are made after you log in to your account, and all loans are subject to our assessment and approval.

  • Step 1: Open an account with Capital Bank of Australia
  • Step 2: Log in to your customer account
  • Step 3: Submit your loan application for assessment
  • Step 4: Receive our decision, subject to credit approval

Not a customer yet? You will need to open an account before you can apply. Existing customers can log in to continue. Approval is not guaranteed, and terms, fees and interest rates apply. Please borrow only what you can reasonably repay.

Help Centre

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about this website.

Can I open a bank account on this website?

Yes. Select Open Account to begin registering for an account with Capital Bank of Australia.

Can I log in or register?

Yes. Select Log In to sign in to your account, or Sign Up to register a new one.

Can I apply for a loan?

Yes, but only if you are a Capital Bank of Australia customer. You must hold an account with us before you can apply. If you do not have an account yet, select Open Account first; existing customers can select Log In to apply. All loans are subject to assessment and approval.

How do I change the language or theme?

Use the language selector and day/dark mode control below the main navigation. Translation requires the external translation service to load.
